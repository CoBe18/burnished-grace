Before you buy

Where are my prices and cabins stored?

In the browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to us. Use Save a backup file now and then, and Restore from a backup to move everything to another computer or browser.

Are the market prices a rule?

No. They are a starting point drawn from published local rates, with the sources shown. Change everything to what you actually charge and pay.

How do I get my download?

After your PayPal payment goes through, the shop gives you your download link and emails it to your PayPal address. For help, email inquiry@BurnishedGraceStudio.com with your receipt.

Before you buy

What do I need?

A Windows or Mac computer with a regular browser window that allows local storage. The desktop instructions use Chrome or Edge. Extract the ZIP and open the included HTML app. Save and reopen a small test recipe before relying on browser storage, and keep regular backups. Private browsing, moving the app file or clearing browser data can affect access to saved recipes.

Can I paste any recipe link?

Link import depends on the source website and the app's hosting setup. Hosted PHP copies can fetch supported public HTTPS recipe pages. Downloaded copies can use the included Recipe Clipper or saved HTML/JSON files. Some sites block importing or do not provide structured recipe data. The app does not bypass paywalls or sign-ins. Photos import when the source allows downloading.

Can I use it on my phone or share with family?

Those features require an HTTPS hosted copy and, for family sharing and sync, a configured cloud account. Hosting is available on request and priced separately. The download does not automatically create a hosted account.

Where are my recipes stored?

The desktop copy stores recipes in your browser on your device. Its Supabase cloud settings are blank. Backups include photos and should be kept separately from the app folder. If you arrange a cloud setup, that setup's account and storage arrangements apply.

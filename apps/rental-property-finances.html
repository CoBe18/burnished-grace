What it does

Everything a rental owner needs to see where the money goes, in one app that never sends your records anywhere.

  • Income and expenses by property

    Record rent and every cost in 20 categories, from management and cleaning to repairs, HOA dues and property taxes.

  • Monthly and yearly results

    See each month's income, expenses and cash flow, plus a yearly summary of net operating income and the bottom line.

  • Five-year cash-flow forecast

    Set rent, vacancy, growth and expense assumptions, and see five years of projected cash flow, returns and debt coverage.

  • Mortgage schedule

    A month-by-month fixed-rate loan schedule, with the option to model extra principal payments.

  • Building depreciation

    Straight-line residential depreciation for the building and improvements, with a 40-year schedule.

  • Reports and backups

    Download CSV reports for your tax professional, and back up or restore everything in one click.

Questions

Where are my records stored?

In your own browser on your own computer. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Download a backup after each session to keep your records safe and to move them to another computer.

Does it work with a property manager or rental program?

Yes. Enter the figures from your monthly owner statement, with rent and each fee in its own category, and the app shows what each property actually earned.

Does it do my taxes?

No. It organizes your records and estimates a tax reserve for planning. Its CSV reports make it easy to share your year with a tax professional.

Can I try it before entering my own records?

Yes. Load the included sample data to explore every feature with three fictional vacation rentals.

Which edition should I choose?

Choose the Individual Edition to track your own rentals. Choose the PLR Edition if you want to sell the app under your own brand. The PLR Edition includes everything in the Individual Edition.

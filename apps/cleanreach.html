Before you buy

What else do I need?

A search key from Geoapify, the map service CleanReach searches. Geoapify offers a free starter plan; you create the account yourself and paste the key into CleanReach. The phone and tablet version needs web hosting you choose, and Sync needs a free Supabase account of your own. Both are optional.

Where are my records kept?

On your own computer. If you turn on Sync, they're also kept in your own Supabase account. They are never sent to us. Download a backup once a month.

Does CleanReach tell me who wants a cleaner?

No. It finds businesses from published map listings and helps you research and contact them. Whether a business needs cleaning is something you confirm yourself, and CleanReach never contacts anyone for you.

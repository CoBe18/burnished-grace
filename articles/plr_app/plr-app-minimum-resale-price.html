Resources for PLR sellers

A PLR App Without a Minimum Price Is Worth Less Every Week You Own It

By Constance Beebe, former hotel general manager with more than 20 years in hospitality

You keep the books for a dozen cabin owners in the Smokies, and every one of them asks you the same thing in November: what did the place really make this year? You find a rental finance app you can buy with private label rights, put your own name on it and sell to every owner on your list. Then you open the license and look for one number before anything else. If there is no minimum resale price, the next person who buys the same rights can list it at a few dollars and set the price for everyone.

This guide is for the bookkeeper, coach or property manager who already serves rental owners and wants a product of their own without building software from scratch. It walks through what to check in a PLR app license, using our own Rental Property Finances PLR Edition as the worked example, because that is the license we can quote word for word.

What does PLR mean for an app?

PLR stands for private label rights: you buy the right to rebrand a finished product and sell it under your own name. With an ebook that means changing the cover and the author line. With an app it should mean much more, because your buyers will open it every month, and they will judge you by how it looks and works.

For an app, ask whether you can change the product name, logo, colors and wording, and whether you get the source code or only a locked file. The Rental Property Finances PLR terms let you change "the product name, logo, colors, tab icon, wording, guides and features," and the PLR Edition includes the complete source code with build and test scripts. It also comes with a no-code Rebrand Tool, so changing the name, logo, colors, fonts and guides does not require you to touch code at all.

Why does a minimum resale price matter on a PLR app?

A minimum resale price stops other license holders from racing yours to the bottom. Unless a license promises exclusivity, assume other buyers hold the same rights. Our terms say so plainly: "Other buyers may hold the same rights, so we cannot promise that your rebranded version will be the only one on the market." Without a floor, the cheapest seller decides what your buyers think the app is worth.

A floor also has to cover the tricks people use to get around it. The Rental Property Finances PLR terms set the minimum at $57.00 USD per copy and apply it "on every platform and in every currency," including discounts, coupons and sales. The same terms rule out "pay what you want" pricing and giving the app away as a free bonus, trial copy or lead magnet. That can feel restrictive on the day you buy. It is what keeps your price believable a year later.

How many copies do I have to sell to earn back a PLR app?

Divide what you paid for the rights by what you keep from each sale. The Rental Property Finances PLR Edition costs $107, and the floor is $57 a copy, so two sales at the minimum bring in $114. That is more than you paid, before your payment processor or sales platform takes its fee. If you price above the floor, you get there faster.

Do the same math on any PLR app before you buy it. A low price for the rights looks like a bargain until you notice there is no floor, and the copies already listed by other buyers are selling for less than you planned to charge.

What should a PLR app license let me do?

It should let you rebrand the product completely, sell individual copies to your customers and keep what you add. Those three are the core, and each one should be written out, not implied. Here is what the Rental Property Finances PLR terms allow:

  • Rebrand the materials completely and publish the result under your own name or business name.
  • Sell individual-use copies to end customers at or above the minimum price.
  • Include the app in a bundle, course or package you sell, as long as its stated value in that offer is at least the minimum price.
  • Use the app yourself for your own rental properties.
  • Use and edit the included sales kit text to market your version.
  • Keep your changes: "Anything you add or change is yours."

That last line matters more than it looks. If you add a report your owners keep asking for, it belongs to you, and it is the thing no other license holder can copy.

What does a PLR app license usually not allow?

A sound PLR license stops you from passing the rights themselves along. Your customers should get a copy to use, not the right to resell it, or the market fills up with copies you never sold. The Rental Property Finances terms say you may not sell or give away private label rights, resale rights or master resale rights, and you may not hand customers the source folder, the Rebrand Tool, the rebranding guide, the sales kit or the PLR terms.

Watch for any rule about claims. Our terms say you may not remove or weaken the app's statements that its tax figures are estimates and not tax, legal or financial advice, or market it as tax preparation software. If you sell to rental owners, that protects you as much as it protects us, because the person your owner calls in April is you.

What do my customers get when I resell a PLR app?

They should get an individual-use license in your business name, so they know who they bought from and what they may do with it. The Rental Property Finances PLR terms require every copy you sell to include a license at least as protective as the included template, and the Rebrand Tool adds that license, in your business name, to every package it creates.

The customer side also means support. The terms put sales, delivery, refunds, questions and support on you, along with the tax collection, consumer protection and privacy laws that apply to your sales. Before you buy any PLR app, open it, load the sample data and make sure you could answer your owners' questions about every screen.

Who is a PLR rental finance app a good fit for?

It fits someone who already has the audience and wants the product. If your clients own long-term or vacation rentals, if some of them are in a resort or rental program and want to check every owner statement, or if they manage their own places and need a simple monthly routine, the app does work they already need. Rental Property Finances runs offline on Windows and Mac desktops and laptops, keeps each owner's records in their own browser on their own computer, and needs no account or subscription.

It is a poor fit if you do not know rental owners yet. A license gives you a product to sell. It does not give you anyone to sell it to.

Questions PLR buyers ask

Can I give a PLR app away as a free lead magnet?

Not under the Rental Property Finances PLR terms. They do not allow giving the app away free, as a free bonus, free trial copy, lead magnet or free item in a membership. Other PLR licenses differ, so read yours before you plan a giveaway.

Can I include a PLR app in a course or bundle?

Under the Rental Property Finances PLR terms, yes, as long as the app's stated value in that offer is at least the $57 minimum price.

Will I be the only person selling this app?

No. PLR rights are non-exclusive, so other buyers may hold the same rights. Your own name, guides, audience and any features you add are what set your version apart.

Who supports my customers after they buy?

You do. Under the Rental Property Finances PLR terms, the reseller is responsible for sales, delivery, refunds, questions and support, and for following the laws that apply to their sales.

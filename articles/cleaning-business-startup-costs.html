What It Really Costs to Start a Small Cleaning Business

Starting a cleaning business can take less money than many businesses, but the costs add up quickly if you don't plan for them. Use this list to build a realistic startup budget.

10 startup expenses to budget for

  1. Business registration and any required licenses
  2. Appropriate business insurance
  3. A dependable vacuum and essential cleaning equipment
  4. Cleaning products suited to the surfaces you will service
  5. Microfiber cloths, mop pads, brushes, and other reusable supplies
  6. Gloves and any additional protective equipment the work requires
  7. Transportation and secure supply storage
  8. A website, business email, and basic branding
  9. Advertising and customer acquisition
  10. Working funds for recurring expenses before customer payments arrive

Your actual startup cost depends on your services, location, and what you already own. Build your budget from current quotes and distinguish one-time purchases from expenses you will pay every month.

Store Policies

Last updated November 17, 2026

Everything in the Burnished Grace shop is a digital download: apps, coloring books, planners and Private Label Rights (PLR) packages. Nothing is shipped. This page explains how your order is delivered, our refund policy, how payment works, and what we do with your information.

Refunds and returns: all sales are final

Because every product is a digital file that is yours the moment it is delivered, all sales are final. We do not offer refunds, returns or exchanges on any product, including single-use editions, PLR editions and bundles.

Please look before you buy. Every app has a walkthrough video on its product page that shows how each function works, and each product page lists what is included, so you know exactly what you are getting.

If something is wrong with your download, we will make it right with a working copy of what you bought. If a download link doesn't work, a file won't open, or you received the wrong file, email inquiry@BurnishedGraceStudio.com with your PayPal receipt and a short description of the problem. We reply within two business days.

Delivery: instant digital download

  • As soon as PayPal confirms your payment, download buttons for each item appear on the page.
  • We also email the download links to the email address on your PayPal account. The email tells you how long the links stay active.
  • Files arrive as ZIP files. Apps include an installation and use guide, and coloring books are printable PDFs.
  • There are no shipping charges and nothing is mailed.
  • If your links don't appear, your email doesn't arrive, or the links have expired, email inquiry@BurnishedGraceStudio.com with your PayPal receipt and we will send your files again.

Prices and payment

Prices are in US dollars and are shown on each product page and in the shop. Checkout runs through PayPal, where you can pay with your PayPal balance, a debit or credit card, or Venmo where PayPal offers it. Your payment details go to PayPal, not to us, so we never see or store your card number.

Licenses

Single-use editions are licensed for your own use and may not be resold or shared. Private Label Rights (PLR) editions come with their own license terms in the download, which explain what you may rebrand and resell. If you have a question about what a license allows, email us before you buy.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to run the shop, and we never sell your information.

  • When you buy: PayPal sends us your name, email address, order number and the items you bought. We use them to deliver your files, answer your questions and keep a record of the sale.
  • When you take our survey: we keep your name, email address and answers. We email you updates only if you ask for them, and every update includes a link to unsubscribe.
  • When you visit: we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. It uses cookies and does not tell us who you are.
  • Free tools: the Budget Ledger stores your entries only in your own browser. We never see them.

To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email inquiry@BurnishedGraceStudio.com.

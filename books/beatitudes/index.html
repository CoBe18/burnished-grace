Questions

Which edition is right for my family?

The Kids Edition is made for ages 1 to 5, with bold, simple pictures. The Teen Devotional Edition adds a short reflection for each Beatitude and questions to journal. The Adult Devotional Edition is the full devotional, with a page for notes and prayers facing every illustration. The Complete Bundle has all three.

Can I print more than one copy?

Yes, for your own household. Each edition is for personal and household use. If you buy the bundle and also want a single edition as a gift for another household, the shop lets you keep both.

Do I have to print the color cover?

No. Where an edition comes with a color cover, it is a separate, optional print file, because it uses a lot of ink.

Which Bible translation is used?

The English editions use the ESV. The Spanish editions use the Reina-Valera 1909.

How do I get my files?

Checkout runs through PayPal. Your download link appears right after payment and is emailed to the address on your PayPal account. If anything goes wrong, email inquiry@BurnishedGraceStudio.com with your receipt.

Before you buy

Does it come with industry prices?

No. The planner works from your own wages, costs and timing, so your prices fit your business rather than someone else's averages.

Where are my records kept?

In your browser on your own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere. Download a full backup at least once a week; it's also how you move your records to another computer.

Does it run payroll or file taxes?

No. It estimates costs for planning and pricing. It doesn't pay people, file taxes or replace your accountant.

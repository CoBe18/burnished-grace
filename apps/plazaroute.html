What it does

Monthly accounts

Each customer’s plan, price, service days, time window, access notes and instructions for your cleaners, with your monthly revenue and estimated profit.

A visit log for every week

Check off each visit as it’s done. Missed, skipped and moved visits stay on record, and visits nobody logged are flagged.

Route days, plaza by plaza

Customers at the same plaza count as one stop. Stops are put in a short driving order from your home base, with directions and a printable route sheet.

Your cleaners’ phones

Each cleaner sees only their own jobs, never your prices. They send before-and-after photos and tap Job complete, and the visit is marked Done for you.

Online bookings

Your own booking link and QR code. Customers book one-time jobs at your open times or ask for monthly service, and you confirm each one.

Paid on your own account

Customers pay on your own Stripe or PayPal link. PlazaRoute never touches the money; you mark each booking Paid.

Revenue and pricing

What each month and week earned, what missed visits put at risk, and a profit check that shows the price to quote before you quote it.

Find the next customer

Search your city for restaurants, salons, gyms, offices and more, keep a prospect notebook, and plan plaza days with the property manager.

Mac, Windows, and a phone and tablet version, with Sync, your team and bookings in your own free Supabase account.

Before you buy

What else do I need?

A free Supabase account of your own for Sync, your team and online bookings, and web hosting you choose for the phone version, your cleaners’ page and your booking page. To find new businesses you need a search key from Geoapify, which offers a free starter plan. You set up each account yourself; the guides walk you through it.

Does PlazaRoute take payments?

No. Customers pay on your own Stripe Payment Link or PayPal link, straight to you. PlazaRoute never touches the money and doesn’t confirm payments for you, so you mark each booking Paid when the money arrives. Refunds are made in your own Stripe or PayPal account.

Does it send texts or emails?

No. It writes the messages, like confirmations and invitations, and you send them yourself by text or email. It also doesn’t notify you when a booking comes in, so check Bookings once or twice a day.

What can my cleaners see?

Only their own jobs: the address, directions, how to get in, your instructions and the customer’s note. Never your prices, your other customers, your prospects or each other.

Where are my records kept?

On your own computer or phone, and in your own Supabase account when you turn on Sync. They are never sent to us. Download a backup once a month.

I already have CleanReach. Do I need both?

No. PlazaRoute does everything CleanReach does, and a CleanReach backup can be restored into PlazaRoute to bring your prospects, plazas and business profile across.

How do I get my download?

After your PayPal payment goes through, the shop gives you your download link and emails it to your PayPal address. For help, email inquiry@BurnishedGraceStudio.com with your receipt.

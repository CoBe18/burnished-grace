A Cleaning Supply Checklist for Your First Jobs

Arriving prepared makes a strong first impression. Gather these supplies and tools before your first jobs, matched to the surfaces and scope of work you'll handle.

12 supplies and tools to prepare

  1. A vacuum suitable for the flooring you will clean
  2. A mop system with clean replacement pads
  3. Color-coded microfiber cloths to help separate cleaning tasks
  4. Non-scratch scrub pads and detail brushes
  5. A suitable general-purpose cleaner
  6. Glass cleaner and lint-free cloths
  7. Bathroom cleaners appropriate for the fixtures and surfaces
  8. Floor cleaners matched to each flooring material
  9. An appropriate disinfectant when the service requires one
  10. Gloves and any protective equipment specified on product labels
  11. Labeled spray bottles, a measuring tool, and a carrying caddy
  12. Trash bags and separate containers for clean and used supplies

Confirm the surfaces and scope of work before packing your kit. Follow product labels, never mix cleaning chemicals, and allow the specified contact time when using a disinfectant.
